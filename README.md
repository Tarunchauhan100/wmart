# W-Mart Privacy Policy
# Effective date: 15 October 2026

Publication note: This is a draft based on the features described for W-Mart. Before publishing, verify that every statement matches the actual released app, its SDKs, permissions, payment flow and backend configuration. Complete the Google Play Data safety form separately and consistently.

# 1. Introduction
This Privacy Policy explains how W-Mart (“W-Mart”, “we”, “our” or “us”) collects, uses, shares and protects information when you use the W-Mart mobile application and related services (the “App”). W-Mart is a hyperlocal shopping and delivery service that may let customers browse products, manage a cart, place and track orders, save delivery addresses, receive notifications and contact support.

By using the App, you acknowledge this Policy. If you do not agree with it, please do not use the App. This Policy should be read together with any applicable Terms of Service, payment terms and return/refund policy.

# 2. Information We Collect
Account and profile information: information you provide when creating or using an account, such as your name, mobile number, email address, profile details and authentication/session information. If you choose Google Sign-In, we may receive the basic profile information that Google makes available after you authorize it, such as your name, email address and profile image.

Location and delivery information: delivery address, city, PIN code, address instructions and associated latitude/longitude coordinates. If you grant location permission, the App may access your device location to help identify your delivery area, set or confirm a delivery address, show nearby service availability or support delivery tracking. You may be able to enter an address manually instead. Background location is not intended to be collected unless a specific feature clearly explains why it is needed and Android displays the relevant permission.

Shopping and order information: products viewed or selected, cart contents, quantities, order details, order history, delivery instructions, order status, cancellation or return/refund requests and communications about an order.

Payment information: payment method, transaction reference, amount, payment status and related records needed to process or reconcile an order. Full payment-card details or UPI credentials should be handled by the relevant payment provider rather than stored by W-Mart, unless the App explicitly tells you otherwise.

Device, app and technical information: device model, operating system and app version, language, diagnostic/crash information, identifiers used for app functionality or security, and basic log data that may be generated when the App communicates with our services.

Notifications and communications: notification token and delivery status where push notifications are enabled, plus information you provide when contacting support.

# 3. How We Use Information
We use information to create and manage accounts; authenticate users; display products and service availability; calculate and fulfil orders; process payments and refunds; deliver purchases; share necessary delivery details with the assigned delivery partner; provide order status and tracking; send order, delivery, security and service notifications; respond to support requests; prevent fraud and abuse; maintain, troubleshoot and improve the App; comply with legal obligations; and enforce our terms and policies.

# 4. Location Permission and Maps
If you grant location permission, W-Mart may use device location to help set your delivery location, verify serviceability and support location-based delivery or tracking features. The App may also use the address and coordinates you save. Maps or navigation features may be provided by third-party services, which may process information under their own privacy policies.

You can manage location permission in your Android device settings. If you deny or disable it, some location-dependent features may not work; you may still be able to enter a delivery address manually if that option is available. The App should request only the location access needed for the feature being used.

# 5. Notifications
If you allow notifications, W-Mart may send order confirmations, order-status updates, delivery updates, important account/service messages and, where offered and permitted, promotional messages. You can turn notifications off in Android settings. Disabling notifications may mean you do not receive timely alerts, but does not necessarily cancel an order or stop essential service messages delivered through other available channels.

# 6. How We Share Information
We may share the minimum information reasonably necessary with: (a) sellers or store operators to prepare and fulfil your order; (b) assigned delivery partners, such as your name, delivery address, contact number, delivery instructions and location needed to complete delivery; (c) payment processors and banks to process transactions, refunds or fraud checks; (d) hosting, database, authentication, storage, notification, maps, analytics or crash-reporting providers that support the App; (e) professional advisers, regulators, law-enforcement agencies or other parties when required by law or necessary to protect rights, safety and security; and (f) a successor entity in connection with a merger, restructuring or transfer of the service, subject to appropriate safeguards.

We use Supabase for backend services such as database, authentication, storage or realtime functionality where configured in the App. Third-party providers process information according to their own terms and privacy policies. We do not sell personal information for money. Do not interpret this statement as a claim that the App uses no advertising or analytics SDKs; those disclosures must match the SDKs actually included in the released build.

# 7. Returns, Cancellations and Refunds
If the App supports cancellations, returns or refund requests, we use the associated order details and communications to assess and process the request. Approval, eligibility, timelines and any exclusions are governed by the applicable W-Mart return/refund and order terms shown to you. Submitting a request does not guarantee that it will be accepted. This Privacy Policy describes information handling and does not replace the separate return/refund policy.

# 8. Data Retention
We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the service, maintain order and transaction records, handle disputes and support requests, meet legal/accounting obligations, prevent fraud and enforce our agreements. Retention periods depend on the type of information and the reason it is needed. When information is no longer required, we will take reasonable steps to delete it or remove identifying details, subject to legal and operational requirements.

# 9. Data Security
We use reasonable technical and organisational measures designed to protect information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. No internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your device and account credentials secure and contact us promptly if you believe your account has been misused.

# 10. Your Choices and Rights
You may be able to view or update profile and saved-address information in the App, manage Android location and notification permissions in device settings, and contact us to request access, correction or deletion of personal information, subject to applicable law and legitimate record-retention requirements.

To request account deletion, email us from an address you can access and include enough information for us to identify the account. Do not send passwords, OTPs, payment credentials or other secrets. We may need to verify your identity before acting. Certain order, payment, tax, fraud-prevention or dispute records may need to be retained where legally required or otherwise permitted.

# 11. Children’s Privacy
W-Mart is not intended for children to use independently without appropriate parental or guardian involvement. We do not knowingly seek to collect personal information from children in violation of applicable law. If you believe a child has provided personal information inappropriately, contact us so we can review the request.

# 12. Third-Party Services
The App may connect to third-party services such as Google Sign-In, payment providers, maps/navigation services, push-notification services and backend hosting providers. Their processing of information is governed by their own policies. Please review the relevant provider policies when using those features.

# 13. Changes to This Policy
We may update this Policy to reflect changes to the App, our practices or legal requirements. We will update the effective date above and may provide an in-App notice or other notice where appropriate. Continued use after an updated Policy becomes effective is subject to the revised Policy, to the extent permitted by law.
